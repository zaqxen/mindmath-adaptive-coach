    Full-score targets in seconds for 1–5 digits: addition 2 / 4 / 7 / 10 / 14; subtraction 2 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 17; multiplication 2 / 6 / 10 / 15 / 21; division 2 / 6 / 11 / 17 / 24; percentages 2 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 17.
